Academics

Faculties & Programmes of Study

Four faculties unite the classical scholarship of the Nalanda tradition with a modern, NEP-2020 aligned curriculum — from philosophy and language to the living science of Sowa Rigpa.

The Four Faculties

Disciplines of study

Philosophy & Logic
Faculty 01

Philosophy & Logic

Classical Buddhist philosophy, epistemology and logic studied through Bhoti and Sanskrit sources in the Nalanda tradition.

  • Madhyamaka & Pramāṇa
  • Bhoti / Sanskrit texts
  • Comparative Philosophy
Language
Faculty 02

Language

Classical and modern languages and literature, preserving the textual heritage of the Himalayan Buddhist world.

  • Bhoti, Sanskrit, Pali
  • Hindi & English
  • Literature & Translation
Sowa Rigpa (Medical Science)
Faculty 03

Sowa Rigpa (Medical Science)

The B.S.R.M.S. programme in the traditional Himalayan science of healing, from materia medica to clinical practice.

  • B.S.R.M.S. degree
  • Traditional pharmacology
  • Clinical training
Modern Studies
Faculty 04

Modern Studies

Sciences and social sciences aligned to NEP-2020, connecting classical scholarship with contemporary disciplines.

  • NEP-2020 aligned
  • Sciences & Social Sciences
  • Skill & Vocational
